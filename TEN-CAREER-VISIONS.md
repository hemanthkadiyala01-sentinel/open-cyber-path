# Ten Career Visions

OpenCyberPath now offers ten learner-selectable structured roadmaps.

1. [SOC Analyst / Blue Team](roadmaps/soc-blue-team.md) — log sources, alert triage, detection basics, and incident notes.
2. [Threat Hunting & Detection Engineering](roadmaps/threat-hunting-detection-engineering.md) — hypotheses, baselines, detections, queries, and evaluation metrics.
3. [Digital Forensics & Incident Response (DFIR)](roadmaps/digital-forensics-incident-response.md) — evidence integrity, event timelines, incident handling, and reporting.
4. [Application Security & Bug Bounty](roadmaps/application-security-bug-bounty.md) — HTTP, common web risks, authorised labs, secure design, and responsible disclosure.
5. [Cloud Security](roadmaps/cloud-security.md) — identity, network boundaries, configuration, logging, and recovery.
6. [GRC & Security Fundamentals](roadmaps/grc-security-fundamentals.md) — risk, control mapping, compliance evidence, and communication.
7. [Penetration Testing & Authorized Red Team](roadmaps/penetration-testing-red-team.md) — scope, rules of engagement, low-impact testing, findings, and remediation.
8. [Security Engineering, DevSecOps & IAM](roadmaps/security-engineering-devsecops-iam.md) — secure architecture, identity, pipeline checks, secure defaults, and recovery.
9. [Threat Intelligence & Ethical OSINT](roadmaps/threat-intelligence-ethical-osint.md) — source reliability, public-data research, indicators, privacy, and intelligence briefs.
10. [Malware Analysis & Reverse Engineering](roadmaps/malware-analysis-reverse-engineering.md) — safe sample handling, metadata, static triage, defensive interpretation, and reports.

Use the [eight-pillar cybersecurity domain map](CYBERSECURITY-DOMAIN-MAP.md) to explore the wider field. The pillars are an overview taxonomy; the ten career visions are the structured learning routes currently provided by the website.
